Good to know.

Who is it being designed for?+

People who find it difficult to get their words into a sentence, including people with speech or language differences. Support for particular communication needs must be evaluated with the people who have those needs.

Will Kendrick speak for me automatically?+

The product is being designed around a review step. You see the suggested sentence, make any changes, and choose when to speak or copy it.

Does the website record my voice?+

No. The demonstrations on this website use written examples. They do not activate your microphone, process speech, or send conversation text to an AI service.
